Why make this Os based on Milkyway Cookie?

I really love Milkyway Cookie since I saw her in her first release!! Afterwards, when I stopped playing Cookiee Run Kingdom as much I was estatic that she was coming too Ovenbreak! Actually, there are many many fans of Milkyway Cookie and DreamJelly Cookie! However there has been a lack in fanmade content for a while, so I wanted to make something for them both as a loveletter to them and to also thank the team at Devsis Ovenbreak department specicfically for making them both in Cookie Run Ovenbreak!
